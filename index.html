About Me

I'm a fullstack developer and founder of CleftProjects, an indie studio dedicated to building purposeful digital products. I specialize in robust Django backends, fast server-driven web UIs with HTMX & Alpine.js, and seamless mobile experiences with Flutter. All my projects are developed, version-controlled, and released under the CleftProjects GitHub organization.

My work spans across robust backend systems, fast web interfaces, and high-performance mobile apps. I prioritize clean architecture, automated testing, and scalable design — ensuring every solution is maintainable, secure, and built to deliver real value from day one. From complex platforms to focused digital tools, every project is approached with the same standard: deliberate planning, clean execution, and long-term impact.
